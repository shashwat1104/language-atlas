Questions

Straight answers to the usual questions.

What is Bhasha IQ and the Language Atlas?+
Bhasha IQ is an India-built research and intelligence company for the global language industry. The Language Atlas is its free knowledge base: a structured, sourced map of the global language industry — from translation and interpreting to language AI, education, data and public infrastructure — organised into 20 volumes and written chapter by chapter.
Who is it for?+
People entering the industry, operators running language businesses, buyers managing localization programmes, and builders looking for problems worth solving. The learning roadmap takes a beginner to expert level in 12 months.
How are facts sourced?+
Every statistic carries its source, year, currency, definition and a confidence tag. Facts, estimates, opinions, hypotheses and forecasts are labelled separately. The source register explains what each major source measures and its limits.
Why do market-size numbers disagree?+
Because Nimdzi, CSA Research and Slator measure different scopes with different methods. Chapter 1 has a full comparison and a rule for using them.
How often is it updated?+
Chapters are added in sequence. The progress table above and the coverage matrix always show what is drafted, what is being researched and what is still only mapped.
How does it treat AI?+
Not as one chapter. Every chapter ends with an AI Impact & Opportunity Brief: a disruption score with evidence, what is automated today, what still needs people, and three buildable ideas.
